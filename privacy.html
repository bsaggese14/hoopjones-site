Hoop Jones Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Hoop Jones ("the app") is made by bsStudios. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

We don't collect your data

The app does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not run any servers for the app.

What stays on your device

Your high score, tour stars, XP, equipped jersey and hair, and sound and haptics settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Apple services

  • Game Center: if you're signed in to Game Center, your arcade score (along with the jersey and hair you scored in) and your tour achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on the leaderboard and in your achievements. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in Settings at any time.
  • In-app purchases: purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
