Hoop Jones

Four shooters, one moving hoop, thirty seconds on the clock. Old-school arcade basketball for iPhone.

Support

Found a bug or have a question? Email bsaggese14@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version.

Common questions

I bought a jersey or hair color but it's gone. Open the Shop and tap Restore while signed in with the same Apple Account.

My scores aren't on the leaderboard. Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in Settings, then finish a full game (all three rounds).

How do I shoot? Tap anywhere in a shooter's column. Time it so the ball reaches the rim as the hoop slides past.
